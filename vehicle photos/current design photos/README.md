this contains our up to date robot and pictures from front,rear,side and top. 

Front the truck has a tfluna in the bumper, with 2 lego pieces acting as a bumper to ensure the tf luna is not the first point of collision in an accident. Then on the roof there is a picam3 wide which we are using for parking, and detecting the objects. Inside the cabin we have our 9v battery and motor controller (like a driver in a normal car) as there was not enough space for everything in the bay of the truck or under the bay. 

In the rear view, you can see our second tf luna connected to the low rear bumper. You can see the raspberry pi 4b connected to all the sensors and ports. Then right behind the cabin is the battery bank(which the camera rests on to give it a slight angle). The battery bank is placed near the centre to improve the centre of gravity of the truck. 

The side view shows the missing door which we have done to give quick and easier access to the motor controller and battery incase

Top view just shows a birds eye view of the robot.
